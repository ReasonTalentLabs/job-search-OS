## How Claude Uses This Document

This document is **advisory**, not prescriptive. When tailoring a resume, Claude reads the
relevant segments below, surfaces the applicable guidance in its edit list, and explains
its reasoning. You always review and approve before any edits are made — and you can
always override any recommendation.

Guidance marked **[DEFAULT]** is a starting assumption, not learned from your data.
Guidance marked **[LEARNED]** has been updated by the `strategy-review` skill based on
actual application outcomes. As your database grows, defaults get replaced with learned signal.

When no segment-specific guidance exists for a role, Claude falls back to the defaults
in the "Baseline Strategy" section.

## Segment Classification Guide

When applying for a role, Claude classifies the opportunity into one primary segment per
dimension. These classifications drive which guidance applies.

**Company size:**
- `Enterprise` — 10,000+ employees, typically publicly traded or F500
- `Large` — 1,000–10,000 employees
- `Mid-market` — 100–1,000 employees
- `SMB/Startup` — under 100 employees, or early-stage funded company

**Role family:**
- `VP-TM` — VP or Sr. Director, Talent Management / Talent Strategy
- `VP-OE` — VP or Sr. Director, Organizational Effectiveness / Org Design
- `VP-HRBP` — VP or Sr. Director, HR Business Partnering
- `VP-HR-Gen` — VP or CHRO of broad HR function (generalist scope)
- `Other` — anything that doesn't fit above

**Industry:**
- `Tech` — software, SaaS, hardware, semiconductors, AI
- `Healthcare` — health systems, pharma, med device, insurance
- `Financial Services` — banking, insurance, fintech, PE/VC
- `Industrial` — manufacturing, energy, defense, engineering
- `Consumer` — retail, CPG, food & beverage, hospitality
- `Other` — anything else

## Baseline Strategy
*Applies when no segment-specific guidance exists. All [DEFAULT] until data says otherwise.*

### Bullet Style
- **[DEFAULT]** Lead with the action and outcome, not the activity. "Reduced manager
  time-to-fill by 28% by redesigning the internal talent review process" not "Responsible
  for talent review process redesign."
- **[DEFAULT]** Every bullet should be attributable to you specifically, not "we" or
  "the team."

### Metrics Density
- **[DEFAULT]** Target 60–70% of bullets with at least one quantified element (%, $, #,
  timeframe, or scale). The remaining 30–40% can be qualitative if the outcome is
  genuinely hard to quantify.
- **[DEFAULT]** Never force a metric that isn't real. A vague number is worse than no number.

### Resume Structure
- **[DEFAULT]** Skills/competencies section at top, below summary. Helps ATS and gives
  recruiters a fast scan before reading bullets.
- **[DEFAULT]** Summary: 3–4 lines. Lead with scope and scale, close with forward hook.
- **[DEFAULT]** 2-page maximum. No exceptions without explicit user override.

### Headline
- **[DEFAULT]** Mirror the JD's exact title. The recruiter should see their req title
  in the first line.

### Tone
- **[DEFAULT]** Executive-direct. No hedging language ("helped," "assisted," "supported").
  No inflated language ("visionary," "transformational"). Clean, confident, specific.

### Resume Guidance
